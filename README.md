# Internship_Telecom

Codes:
- **TW_ab**: defines a class to solve the max of Bell Inequalities in the TW, with two Observers: Alice and Bob (TW1)
- **TW_abe**: defines a class to solve the max of Bell Inequalities in the TW, with three Observers: Alice, Bob and Eve (TW1)
- **TW_ab_noTW_e**: defines a class to solve the max of Bell Inequalities in the TW, with three Observers: Alice, Bob and Eve. Alice and Bob
  have the constraints of TW, but Eve doesn't. It is possible to add the constraint of Bound Randomness. (TW1)
- **TW_triplets**: defines a class to solve the max of Bell Inequalities in the TW, with three Observers: Alice, Bob and Eve. Alice and Bob 
  have the constraints of TW with three triplets, but Eve doesn't. It is possible to add the constraint of Bound Randomness. (TW1)
- **TW_ab_noTW_e**: defines a class to solve the max of Bell Inequalities in the TW, with three Observers: Alice, Bob and Eve. Alice and Bob have the constraints of TW, but Eve doesn't. It is possible to add the constraint of Bound Randomness. (TW2, TW3)
